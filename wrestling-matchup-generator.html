Event Setup

Guided workflow:
  1. Add event details.
  2. Import or manually enter wrestlers.
  3. Review and clean roster.
  4. Set matchup preferences.
  5. Generate and review rounds and flags.
  6. Export CSV or print to PDF.
Experience level definitions:
Beginner: New to the sport, typically year 1, with introductory skills.
Intermediate: 1 or more years of experience with progressing skills.
Advanced: 2 or more years of experience with advanced skills.

Unmatched wrestlers

Coach guidance and review flags

  • Same-school: matchup includes wrestlers from the same school.
  • Weight over preferred: exceeds preferred weight or percent threshold.
  • Weight over absolute: exceeds absolute threshold (only allowed when exceptions are enabled).
  • Beginner vs Advanced: largest experience gap fallback.
  • Grade gap > 2: larger grade difference fallback.
  • Rematch: pair has previously met in this event across rounds.
